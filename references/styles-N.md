# Style group N — Natural warm (read only when the chosen style is N1–N3)

## N — Natural warm

### N1. Organic / Nature Distilled — wellness, clinics, tea, food and drink, sustainability
- Earthy palette (moss, clay, sand, stone, oat), natural textures (paper, linen), organic curves and botanical line illustrations; warm serif + humanist sans (`Fraunces`/`Cormorant Garamond` + `DM Sans`/`Nunito Sans`, with `Noto Serif SC`/`Noto Sans SC`). Motion: gentle, breathing.

### N2. Vintage Analog / Retro Film — photography, cafes, travel, storytelling
- Warm faded colours, film grain, light leaks, polaroid or contact-sheet frames, date stamps; `DM Serif Display`, `Courier Prime` + `Noto Serif SC`. Motion: slow fades, slight frame jitter on hover.

### N3. Claymorphism — games, kids, cute apps, playful tools
- Soft 3D clay-like shapes, pastel colours, double soft shadows (outer + inner), radius 24–32px, rounded friendly type (`Fredoka`, `Baloo 2`, `Nunito` + `Noto Sans SC` rounded weights). Motion: bouncy springs (gentle), squish on press.
