# App and dark mode (read only when building an app screen or a dark mode)

## E10. App and dark mode (only when needed)
- Native idioms: iOS tab bar and swipe-back; Android top app bar and predictive back; respect safe areas and dynamic text size.
- Dark mode: design light and dark together; dark uses desaturated, lighter tonal variants (not inverted colours) — in OKLCH keep H, raise L and lower C for accents; check contrast separately for both.
