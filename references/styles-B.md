# Style group B — Bold expressive (read only when the chosen style is B1–B5)

## B — Bold expressive

### B1. Swiss Industrial Print (brutalist light) — fitness, trades and construction, manifestos
- Paper `#F4F4F0`/`#EAE8E3`, carbon ink `#050505`–`#111111`, ONE hazard red `#E61919`/`#FF2A2A`; no gradients, soft shadows or translucency.
- Heavy neo-grotesk macro type (`Archivo Black`, `Archivo` 900, `Anton`, `Bebas Neue` + `Noto Sans SC` 900), `clamp(4rem,10vw,15rem)`, tracking -0.03 to -0.06em, leading 0.85–0.95, Latin uppercase; micro mono text (`JetBrains Mono`, `IBM Plex Mono`, `Space Mono`) 13–14px, tracking 0.05–0.1em.
- Blueprint grid with visible 1–2px compartments (`display:grid; gap:1px` over a contrasting parent), bimodal density, viewport-bleeding numerals; **all corners 90°**; ASCII framing `[ ]`, `>>>`, `///`, ® © ™ marks, crosshairs, barcodes, warning stripes, spec strings tied to real content; global noise; halftone images. Motion: hard snaps, ticking counters, lines drawing in.

### B2. Neubrutalism — young brands, startups, events, games, creators
- Bright flat colours (yellow, pink, mint, cobalt from a triadic set) on off-white, thick black 2–3px borders, hard offset shadows (`4px 4px 0 #111`, allowed here), radius 0 or 8–12px, chunky grotesk (`Space Grotesk`, `Archivo`, `Lexend` + `Noto Sans SC` bold). Buttons press down into their shadow on `:active`. Motion: snappy, playful.

### B3. Kinetic Typography — events, music, campaigns, launches
- Type is the image: variable-weight words that scale, stretch or slide with scroll, marquees, split letters (`Clash Display`, `Anton`, `Archivo` variable + `Noto Sans SC` 900). Two colours max. Motion high, always with reduced-motion fallback.

### B4. Bauhaus — education, art, culture, kids' learning
- Primary red/yellow/blue + black on off-white; circles, squares and triangles as layout elements; geometric sans (`Outfit`, `Josefin Sans`, `Archivo`) + `Noto Sans SC`; strong grid, asymmetric balance. Motion: shapes sliding into place.

### B5. Memphis — parties, kids, playful creative brands (use sparingly)
- Pastel + bold mix, squiggles, dots, confetti shapes, patterned blocks, rounded playful type (`Fredoka`, `Baloo 2` + `Noto Sans SC`). Keep content areas calm so text stays readable.
